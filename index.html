Active SMS Safeguards
  • Sending hours: SMS can only be sent between 9 AM and 9 PM Perth time (AWST). Sends outside this window are blocked entirely.
  • Opt-out filter: Any recipient who replied STOP is permanently excluded from all campaigns and automations.
  • 7-day frequency cap: A customer who already received a campaign in the past 7 days will not receive another one.
  • Deduplication: If the same phone number appears more than once in a batch, it is only sent to once.
  • AU phone validation: Only valid Australian mobile numbers (+61 followed by exactly 9 digits) can receive SMS. Customers with missing, malformed, or non-AU numbers are silently skipped across all send paths (campaigns, automations, and feedback).
  • Campaign send limit: If a campaign targets more recipients than the limit set above, the Send button is disabled until you manually type the exact count to confirm.
